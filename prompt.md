H1: About Zento — Nurse-Led Care, Founded by Nurses
Zento Group is a nurse-led care-at-home provider supporting adults across Edinburgh, East, Mid and West Lothian, and Perthshire, including Crieff and Comrie. We help people live safely, comfortably and independently in their own homes — from a weekly friendly visit to comprehensive, round-the-clock support.
Why we started Zento
Zento was founded by practising nurses whose careers were spent in the NHS, on acute wards, and in long-term care homes, supporting hundreds of older people through dementia, Parkinson's, stroke, frailty and the final chapters of life. One wish came up again and again: "I just want to go home." Zento exists to answer that wish — bringing genuinely good, clinically-informed care into the place people feel most themselves.
Our values

Kindness — every person is treated with warmth, patience and genuine compassion.
Respect — your home, your choices, your routines. We fit around you.
Reliability — we turn up when we say we will, and do what we promise.
Dignity — personal care delivered sensitively, protecting your privacy at all times.
Partnership — we work with you, your family and other professionals as one team.
Led by nurses, accountable to you
Day-to-day care is led by our Registered Manager, Adnan Karim RGN, alongside a dedicated Care Quality Manager — also a practising registered nurse — who provides independent oversight of standards. Both report through a Board-level Director of Care Standards, so quality assurance never rests with one person alone.
Regulated and accountable
Zento Group is registered with the Care Inspectorate, Scotland's independent care regulator (Support Service registration CS2026000326; Housing Support Service registration CS2026000325; provider number SP2025000950), and we deliver care in line with the Health and Social Care Standards (Scotland) 2017. Every carer is PVG-checked, SSSC-registered, and trained through a structured, competency-based induction.
Where we work
We're proud to serve the City of Edinburgh, East, Mid and West Lothian, and Perthshire — including Crieff, Comrie, Muthill and Braco — from our two local offices in Edinburgh and Crieff.
